Contact us

Email support@woolyback.com. Please say which device you play on and what happened; a screenshot helps.

I bought the full game and it is locked again

On iPhone or iPad, open the Full game page and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. On Android the purchase comes back by itself once you are signed in to Google Play with the account you bought it with.

Refunds

Refunds are given by the shop you bought from (Apple, Google or Steam), not by us. Each has a refund request page in your purchase history.

My progress has gone

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the game, or choosing Clear all progress in Settings, removes it, and we have no copy to give back.
