Privacy policy

Compulsory Purchase, a game by Woolyback LLC. Effective 25 November 2026.

The short version

The game does not collect, send or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Your progress, your settings and any maps you make in the map editor are saved on your own device (or, on Steam, in your Steam save folder). Woolyback LLC never receives them. Deleting the game, or choosing Clear all progress in Settings, removes them.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any device identifier, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases and game services

If you buy the full game, the purchase is handled entirely by the shop you bought it from (Apple's App Store, Google Play or Steam). We do not see your payment details. If you use the shop's game service for achievements or leaderboards (Game Center, Google Play Games or Steam), that service is run by its owner under its own privacy policy, and it records what you earn there.

Map codes

A map you make can be turned into a text code. The code holds the map and nothing about you. It leaves your device only if you copy it and send it to someone yourself.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, children included.

Asking us to delete your data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Everything the game saves is on your device and under your control.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@woolyback.com.
